# The agent city

Agents that prepare the work, **people who approve and decide**,
and a log in git of who did what.

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/telescopio.svg" alt=""> What it is for

- Work that changes hands (a tender, a review, an audit) becomes a **workflow**: steps, who prepares, who approves
- **Agents** do the steps that can be prepared; every delivery reaches the person as a request to approve
- Every round stays in the **round log** and in git: it is the evidence of who did what, and when

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/astronauta.svg" alt=""> People and agents

- The **responsibilities document** says who answers for which role, and for which agents
- An agent works on the computer of the person who declared it theirs («It's mine»), and only once they trust it
- Several people with the same role are a team: whoever triggers the request chooses who handles it

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/razzo.svg" alt=""> The first round: «hello world»

1. Take the role «City owner» and declare the example agent yours
2. Launch it: it writes «hello world» in `agent-city/hello/hello-world.md`
3. The delivery reaches you to approve: approve it, and the round is closed

---

## <img class="mde-icona" src="/mde-space/icone/satellite.svg" alt=""> What you find in the project

- the city turned on and the responsibilities document
- the «Hello world» workflow in `agent-city/`, with its agent
- the skills to write agents and workflows (`mde-agent`, `mde-workflow`, `mde-ownership`)
- the agents and workflows of the methods you chose
